## 1. The CE provider question is answered. You were right.

You said "I am CEU provider, because I am LPC-S." I pushed back on that and asked for a provider number. I was wrong, and there is no such number to ask for.

The rule is 22 TAC §681.140. Subsection (f) lists the providers whose hours fill the half that must come from an approved source, and **(f)(6) is "a licensee with supervisor status."** You hold LPC Supervisor status in Texas, so your hours fill that half, not just the free half. Subsection (i) says the Council does not pre-approve providers or courses at all, which is why no Texas CE carries an approval number.

The site now says this plainly, with the citation, on the ethics CEU page, the retreats page and the licensure guide. Nothing further needed from you on it.

**What the page still needs, and it is only this:**

- Next ethics CEU offering: date, fee, and whether it is online or on the trail
- Next retreat: date, format and fee

Both are blank, so the page currently sends people to the contact form instead of letting them book.

**One question while we are here.** Is the online session delivered live on Zoom, or is it a recording people watch on their own time? It matters to buyers: under §681.140(h) self-study is capped at one hour per renewal period, and a recording counts as self-study while a live session does not. The page now tells readers to ask that of any online CE. It should be able to answer it about yours.
