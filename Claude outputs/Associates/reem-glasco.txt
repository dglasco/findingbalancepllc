SUMMARY

Reem Glasco is a Licensed Professional Counselor Supervisor in Texas, license 66562,
with over fifteen years of clinical experience and supervisor status since 2009. She is a
NARM therapist, a Somatic Experiencing Practitioner (SEP), EMDR certified and an EMDRIA
Approved Consultant. She works with young adults, college-age clients and adults, on
developmental trauma, C-PTSD, anxiety, panic attacks, depression, and the kind of
procrastination that does not respond to discipline. She was a Clinical Partner at Capital
Area Counseling from 2021 to 2025, and practices from Austin, Texas: online across the
state, and outdoors when the work asks for more than a screen.

HOW I WORK

Most of what brings people here was learned somewhere, and learned for a reason. All three
methods I work with start from that, and none of them asks you to push through.

NARM is an invitation to get curious about a pattern rather than to fight it. Where did I
learn this? What was it doing for me then? And if I am present enough to notice it
happening, what choice do I actually have? The answers tend to arrive through the felt
sense rather than through analysis, which is why the work is slower than advice and why it
holds.

EMDR, as I practice it, is IFS-informed: the protective parts of you are consulted before
anything is processed, and most of my emphasis sits in the preparation, where that
agreement gets built. It is weighted toward stabilization rather than excavation, which is
what acute stress, ongoing stress, overwhelm and loss actually need.

Somatic Experiencing meets shock trauma a little at a time, slow and gentle, building your
window of tolerance rather than testing it. A nervous system learns resilience at the pace
it can hold, and not faster.

I teach effortless self-care through walk and mindful therapy, outdoors, which for some
people is the difference between talking about something and being able to stay with it.

For therapists: I have supervised LPC Associates since 2009, online anywhere in Texas, and
I run mindful trail retreats for interns, associates and therapists, where we look at being
present and at what gets in the way of it.
