Hi! My name is Nica and I work with individual adults, ages 25-55.

You are looking for happiness and purpose but you are feeling drained and overwhelmed. You want to change but maybe you don’t know how to make that happen or even where to start.

You are having difficulty figuring out where you belong. Your values and aspirations don’t align with what you see other people doing. You might need help identifying unmet needs or maybe you feel like you need to break away from things that no longer resonate with you.

You feel like you don’t have a space to discuss the difficult aspects of your life. You don’t want to be judged or feel like a burden, but you don’t want to feel isolated and alone either. You’re looking to strengthen your ability to communicate and connect with other people.

I invite you to take up space.

I approach therapy with genuine curiosity about who my clients are and a belief in their inherent value. I try to create a relaxed and trust-based space where my clients can guide the way to explore anything they want. I am sex-positive, weirdo-friendly, shame-free towards addiction, and an LGBTQIA+ ally.

Ask me about nature-based therapy and how it’s used in the healing process!
