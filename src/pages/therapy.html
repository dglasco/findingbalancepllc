What people bring here

  • Developmental trauma: patterns that formed early, before you had words or choices.
  • C-PTSD and PTSD, including trauma that is ongoing rather than finished.
  • Anxiety, panic attacks, and a body that stays braced when nothing is happening.
  • Depression, flatness, and a sense that something is off you cannot point at.
  • Procrastination that is not laziness and does not respond to discipline.
  • Life transitions: graduating, leaving, arriving, becoming a parent, losing someone.
  • Self care for mothers, which usually starts with permission.

Who this is for

Adults and young adults, including college-age clients. The practice does not work with young children.

Trail walk, movement and equine sessions are for adults only, and need a consult plus one to three online sessions first, so we know each other before we are outside.

Common questions about therapy here

How much does a therapy session cost?

$240 for a 50-minute online session with Reem, private pay. Trail walk and movement sessions are $240 to $370 depending on length. The practice's LPC Associates see clients at lower fees, including sliding scale.

Do you take insurance?

The practice is private pay and is not accepting new Blue Cross Blue Shield clients. Payment is by major credit card. If you plan to seek out-of-network reimbursement, raise it in the consult so you know what documentation to expect.

Is therapy in person or online?

Online across Texas is the default. In person means trail walk and movement therapy on Fridays, or equine sessions near Dripping Springs, both for adults, both after a consult and one to three online sessions.

What happens in a first session?

Mostly orientation: what brought you, what you want different, what your nervous system does under stress, and how we will pace the work. Nothing about trauma gets opened faster than you can stay present for.

How long will therapy take?

It depends what we are working with. Stabilisation can be a handful of sessions. Developmental trauma is slower, because we are building capacity rather than processing a single event. We review it together instead of guessing at the start.

What if I need help sooner than you have openings?

Ask about the associates, who generally have availability sooner and lower fees. If neither fits, Reem will refer you out rather than leave you on a waitlist. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.
