Who tends to work well here

The associate who wants more than hours

You could get cheaper supervision. What you want is someone who will tell you what actually happened in that session, and help you build something after licensure.

The therapist whose EMDR keeps stalling

Preparation looks fine, then processing floods or flatlines. Usually that is a part of the client objecting, and it is workable once you can see it.

The clinician running on empty

Competent, respected, quietly depleted. You teach regulation skills you no longer practise. That is what the retreats are for.

The one drawn to body-based work

You have read about somatic approaches and want to practise inside one. NARM, Somatic Experiencing and IFS-informed EMDR are the working lens here, not electives.

Questions therapists ask first

Are you accepting new LPC Associates?

One supervision spot opens in fall 2026 and one in spring 2027. Supervision is capped deliberately, so it is worth booking a consult before a spot opens rather than after.

Can I do EMDR consultation without being supervised by you?

Yes. Consultation is separate from supervision and open to any licensed therapist trained in EMDR, at $185 an hour online. LPC Associates under Reem's supervision get individual EMDR consults at no additional cost.

Do I have to be in Texas?

For LPC supervision, yes: the supervised experience requirement is set by the Texas Behavioral Health Executive Council and applies to Texas LPC Associates. EMDR consultation is a peer professional relationship rather than clinical care, so it is not limited to Texas, though you should check what your own board expects.

What is the first step?

A free thirty-minute consult by Zoom or phone. For supervision, applications then need a CV, three professional references, and answers to the seven questions listed on the supervision page.
