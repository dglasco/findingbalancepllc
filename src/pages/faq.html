Getting started

How do I become a client?

Email the practice to request a free 15-minute consult. If it is a fit, you receive client forms and we schedule. If it is not, you get a referral rather than a polite brush-off.

Where is Finding Balance Counseling located?

The practice is based in Cedar Park, Texas, and works online with clients anywhere in the state, including Austin. In-person trail walk sessions happen outdoors in central Texas, and equine sessions are near Dripping Springs.

Should I see Reem or one of the associates?

Usually it comes down to availability and fee. Reem keeps a small therapy caseload alongside her supervision and consultation work, at $240 a session. The LPC Associates she supervises have more open calendars and lower fees, including sliding scale, and practise under her supervision.

Who does Reem work with?

Young adults, college-age clients and individual adults, plus LPC Associates and licensed therapists in supervision or consultation. The practice does not work with young children.

What are the office hours?

By appointment. Walk and table therapy sessions are on Fridays.

Money

How much is a session?

$240 for a 50-minute online session, private pay. Trail walk and movement sessions run $240 to $370 depending on length. Sliding-scale fees are available through the practice's LPC Associates.

Do you accept insurance?

Sessions are private pay and the practice is not accepting new Blue Cross Blue Shield clients. Payment is by major credit card.

Cancel telehealth appointments at least 24 hours ahead, or an $85 fee may apply. In-person walk therapy is paid in advance and is non-refundable inside 48 hours.

The work itself

What kind of therapy does Reem practise?

NARM for the patterns formed early in life, Somatic Experiencing for what the nervous system is still holding, and EMDR for specific traumatic material once there is enough stability. IFS-informed and Brainspotting elements are used where parts of a person disagree about the work.

What is walk and talk therapy?

A therapy session held outdoors while walking, with nature and movement underneath the conversation. It runs 75 minutes to two hours, is for adults only, and requires a consult plus one to three online sessions first. Fees are $240 to $370, paid in advance.

Do you work with horses?

Yes. Equine-assisted sessions are available near Dripping Springs, Texas, for clients, for LPC associates in supervision at $60 a session, and as optional training for therapists who want to work with horses themselves.

For therapists

How many supervised hours do I need for a Texas LPC?

3,000 clock-hours under a Council-approved supervisor, at least 1,500 of them direct client counselling contact, over no fewer than 18 months, with a minimum of four supervision hours a month and no more than half of your total supervision hours in a group. The licensure guide on this site works through all of it, including the 60-month expiry on your Associate licence.

Do you run retreats or training for therapists?

Yes. An annual mindful trail retreat for interns, associates and licensed therapists, equine-assisted sessions and training near Dripping Springs, and periodic ethics continuing education for Texas LPCs. Associates in supervision with Reem are included in the annual retreat.

Are you accepting LPC Associates for supervision?

One supervision spot opens in fall 2026 and one in spring 2027. Supervision is $425 a month for three individual hours and one group hour, online and statewide in Texas. Applications start with a free 30-minute consult.

Can my agency contract supervision for a short period?

Yes. Three-month contracts are available for associates working within an agency or group practice at $500 a month.

Can you be my EMDR consultant?

Reem is an EMDRIA Approved Consultant. Individual consultation is $185 an hour online, focused on IFS-informed EMDR for phases one and two, and it is free for her own LPC associates.

Do you offer ethics CEUs?

Ethics continuing education for Texas LPCs is offered periodically. Email the practice to ask about the next session.

Safety

What if I am in crisis right now?

This practice does not provide emergency care. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or call 911 if you are in immediate danger. Reach out here once you are safe and we can talk about ongoing support.
